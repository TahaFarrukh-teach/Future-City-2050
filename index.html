🎮 How to Play

Create up to 4 worlds. Choose a world type, then make one decision in each city category. Every choice changes your city's stats. Try different combinations and generate your City Report. Your world is saved in this browser.

ℹ️ About

Future City 2050 is a strategy-style browser game about designing a fictional future city. There is no right answer. Balance technology, people, money, nature and the environment to create your best future.
